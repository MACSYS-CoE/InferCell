Prediction

Source: src/predict.jl, src/posterior_draws.jl, src/predict_completion.jl, src/predict_forward.jl, src/predict_viability.jl, src/predict_intervention.jl.

The entry point

predict computes the predictive distribution of a quantity Q, given the data, as dev/notes/2026-10-29-cut-predictive-distribution.md defines it. Its arguments keep five things apart:

  • the posterior source, a PosteriorDraws;
  • the forward description: forward = :hybrid or :mean_field, with delta for the mean field;
  • the completion policy: completion = :none, :prior or :viability_conditioned_prior, with held, width, n_held and viability;
  • the estimand, the population mean of Q over cells;
  • the intervention.

Its layout is one crossed matrix: posterior draws θI as rows, held draws θH as columns, the held draws shared by every row. Each pair runs its own fresh cells, since the Monte Carlo correction E[σ²]/n holds only if no two draws share cell seeds. An intervention's arm reuses its pair's seeds, so the effect is paired within a draw. The Prediction keeps every run, every decision and every seed. Its summary separates the spread of μ̂ over the pairs from the Monte Carlo term and from the cell-to-cell spread. Without completion, their difference is the parameter variance. With completion it is not: every row shares the same held draws, so about 1/K of the held parameters' share is missing from it for K held draws, and all of it at one held draw. Its estimator on the crossed matrix, like the θI/θH split's, is fixed in M4.

What predict leaves to the freeze (KIM-36, KIM-39): which Q, the viability rule, the dose, which conditioned variant is the headline, and the estimator for the θI/θH variance split. It takes the first three as arguments, and returns both variants' weights and the matrix the split needs.

On M0 no parameter is held, so completion = :none. That is the scored case. On the reduced model the held parameters come from their priors, and the result is unscored: no truth matches those data.

The toy models' posterior_predictive reads its posterior the same way, and forks the rng the same way. It draws without replacement, or by weight with replacement from an ABC posterior. It runs the toy composition once per draw, an ODE solve or, for a jump module, a stochastic run, and returns the trajectories.

Reading a posterior

posterior_draws reads any posterior InferCell writes into one PosteriorDraws: the exact route's V7 replicate files, the mixed route's B7 chain files, the Chains that infer(models, ds; route) returns, and the toy models' Chains and ABCPosterior. Every column is mapped to its parameter by name, and every value is held on the natural scale, ready to write into a model. The bulk noise σ_b is kept in its own field: it is observation noise, not a cell parameter, and a forward simulation never reads it.

Completing the held parameters

On the reduced model, M0's posterior covers five parameters, and every other free parameter is held: no data reaches it. held_parameters lists them for a composition, skipping the slots the driver overwrites (the enz_R_* catalytic slots), the reverse constants, and point priors, and says why each was skipped. draw_held draws them from their priors, which width widens or narrows about their medians for the sensitivity runs. Reverse constants are derived from the whole set that runs, posterior and held values together, by haldane_values, so every reaction keeps its equilibrium constant.

Forward descriptions and δ

forward_cells runs a composition at given parameter writes, read for the predicted quantity Q:

  • forward = :hybrid runs the reference composition, one stochastic cell per seed, and each seed fixes its cell;
  • forward = :mean_field runs route (ii), one ODE, with no cells and no single-cell distribution.

Q is read from the run's metered lactate export, so every composition is metered first: :lactate_out is the lactate exported in particles and :exported is the fraction of the lactate formed that left the cell. A function of the run is accepted too. Each run is kept as a CellRun: its Q, its clips, the smallest particle count of each state, and its failure, if it failed, with the reason.

The mean field has no δ for Q of its own. The mixed route's δ covers only its 17 panel pools. delta_correction computes one from a hybrid ensemble at a reference point, once, and keeps it as a DeltaCorrection to reuse.

Viability

A viability rule V is data, a ViabilityRule, and nothing in the code fixes its thresholds. V is frozen in M4, before any prediction is read. cell_status puts each run in one of five categories, which are not interchangeable: failed, starved, below a floor, clipped, or clean. V is a property of θ: assess_viability accepts a parameter set when enough of its cells are viable, and otherwise keeps its count of cells per category as the reason. The hybrid reads V on the same cells that give μ̂, so where Q differs between viable and unviable cells, and the viable fraction is near the threshold, the conditioned μ̂ is biased. Whether V reads cells of its own is fixed with V. Over the crossed matrix of posterior draws × held draws, viability_weights gives both conditioned variants of the methods note as weights: (b) keeps θI's marginal as the posterior's, and (a) lets the filter act on θI. Each comes with its acceptance rate and effective sample size.

Interventions

An Intervention is applied after the parameter draw. The first is a Knockdown of the ptsG promoter, which scales S_JCVISYN3A_0779 by 1 − dose and changes nothing else (intervene). It assumes the intervention acts only through that parameter, and that the posterior fitted on unperturbed data carries over. intervention_cells runs both arms on the same seeds, so each cell is paired with itself and the effect ΔQ keeps little of the intrinsic noise. A dose of 0 gives ΔQ = 0 exactly. delta_correction covers the intervened arm too.

InferCell.Prediction — Type
Prediction

What predict returns. Rows are posterior draws (m = 1…M), columns held draws (k = 1…K). Without completion there is one column.

The draws:

  • draws: each row's index into the posterior;
  • theta_I: each row's posterior values;
  • sigma_b: each row's σ_b, carried and never simulated, or nothing;
  • held and theta_H: the held names, and each column's drawn values;
  • seeds: the cell seeds, n_cells × K × M. They are empty for the mean field.

The runs:

  • cells: every run, M × K, each a vector of CellRun;
  • mu: μ̂_mk, the mean of Q over each matrix cell's finished runs. For the mean field this is m(θ), uncorrected;
  • sigma2: σ̂²_mk, the sample variance of Q over those cells. It is NaN for the mean field, which has no single-cell distribution, and below two cells;
  • n_ok: the runs that finished.

The intervention, when one is given:

  • cells_intervened: the paired runs, on the same seeds. Pairs that V rejected are not run;
  • effect and effect_sigma2: the paired effect Δ's mean and variance.

The conditioning:

  • viability: the rule;
  • viable: V's decision per pair, all true without a rule;
  • decisions: each pair's (; viable, fraction, counts) (assess_viability), so a rejected pair keeps its reason;
  • weights: the weight sets over the matrix, uniform, or per_theta_I and joint under V (viability_weights), each with its acceptance and ESS in conditioning.

The results:

  • delta: the mean field's DeltaCorrection, or nothing;
  • summary and effect_summary: each weight set's population-mean results. See predict;
  • statuses: the count of every baseline run in each CELL_STATUSES category, read without a rule. This is the unfiltered ensemble's validity diagnostic;
  • provenance: what was run, and from what (KIM-33):
    • fork_seed: the seed of the stream forked from rng, from which every draw, held value and cell seed came;
    • version: InferCell's;
    • settings: the call's arguments, as run;
    • source: the posterior's;
    • reductions: the composition's declared reductions, the driver's or route (ii)'s included (reduction_declarations);
    • skipped: the parameters the completion left out, and why (held_parameters);
    • pairs: one record per pair, M × K. Each gives the posterior draw (index, chain, iteration), where θH came from (:none, :prior or `:viabilityconditioned`), its seeds, V's decision, and every failed run with its seed and reason.

StatsAPI.predict — Method
predict(models, posterior::PosteriorDraws; horizon, forward = :hybrid,
        completion = :none, held = nothing, width = 1.0, n_held = 1,
        viability = nothing, quantity = :lactate_out, estimand = :population_mean,
        intervention = nothing, delta = :none, theta_ref = nothing, n_ref = 100,
        rng = Random.default_rng(), n_parameter_draws = length(posterior),
        draws = nothing, n_cells = 1) -> Prediction

The predictive distribution of the quantity Q given the data, for the composition models, as dev/notes/2026-10-29-cut-predictive-distribution.md defines it. The composition is metered first.

The posterior source is a PosteriorDraws (posterior_draws). n_parameter_draws of its draws are used, chosen without replacement (or by weight, for an ABC posterior), or the indices draws exactly. σ_b is carried beside each draw and never simulated. Two calls with the same rng draw the same held values and the same cell seeds whatever their draws. So splitting one prediction's rows over calls that share an rng reruns the same cells on different rows. Calls with different rngs draw different held values, and do not form one crossed matrix.

The forward description:

  • forward = :hybrid runs n_cells stochastic cells for each parameter set;
  • forward = :mean_field runs one route (ii) solve.

For the mean field, delta = :ensemble adds δ from a hybrid ensemble of n_ref cells at theta_ref (delta_correction). The default theta_ref is the posterior mean of log θ_I, with every held parameter at its nominal value. A DeltaCorrection computed once can be passed as delta instead, and is checked against this prediction. The uncorrected mean field is kept beside the corrected one, since the gap between them is part of the result (spec §12 2026-10-29, O4).

The completion policy:

  • completion = :none runs the posterior as it stands, as on M0, where no parameter is held;
  • completion = :prior completes the held parameters (by default held_parameters) from their priors, width times as wide. n_held = K draws are crossed with every posterior draw;
  • completion = :viability_conditioned_prior does the same, and then reads the rule viability (a ViabilityRule) on each pair's baseline runs. Both conditioned variants are returned as weights (viability_weights), with each pair's decision and reason. For the hybrid, V is read on the same cells that give μ̂. So where Q differs between viable and unviable cells, and a θ's viable fraction is near the rule's threshold, the conditioned μ̂ is biased. Whether V should read cells of its own is part of fixing V (M4).

The estimand is :population_mean, the mean of Q over cells, μ(θ). For each weight set, summary gives:

  • mean: the predicted population mean, δ-corrected when δ is given;
  • var: the spread of μ̂ over the matrix's pairs;
  • mc: E[σ̂²]/n, the Monte Carlo part of that spread, which the hybrid's finite cells add;
  • parameter_var: var − mc. A negative difference is reported as 0, and flagged. Under two finished cells per pair (the default n_cells = 1 among them), σ̂² does not exist, so mc and parameter_var are NaN and mc_undefined is set;
  • intrinsic_var: E[σ̂²], the cell-to-cell spread (hybrid only). It is a separate output, not part of the estimand's uncertainty;
  • ess, n_pairs, and n_no_q, the pairs with no finished run.

Without completion, the rows are independent posterior draws, and parameter_var estimates the parameter variance. With completion it does not: every row shares the same K held draws, so with equal weights the held parameters' main effect enters var only in proportion M(K−1)/(MK−1), about (K−1)/K, and not at all at n_held = 1. Then var is a spread over the matrix, not the parameter variance. The total and its θI/θH split are not computed here, since their estimators on the crossed matrix are fixed in M4 (note §5.2). mu, sigma2, viable and the weights are what they need.

The intervention (an Intervention, such as a Knockdown) is applied after the parameter draw. Its arm runs on the same seeds, and effect_summary gives the paired effect ΔQ. V is read at baseline only. ΔQ is taken over the cells that finish in both arms. A cell that fails only under the intervention leaves ΔQ. It is listed in its pair's failed_intervened (provenance) and not counted in statuses.

The cells reseed the global generator. predict draws everything from a stream forked from rng and puts the global generator back afterwards, as infer does. Two calls with equal rng states give the same result. The fork seed is recorded, but predict cannot be called with one. To replay a call, keep the rng's seed and the commit.

InferCell.PosteriorDraws — Type
PosteriorDraws

A posterior's draws over named model parameters, as predict reads them, whatever produced them. Build one with posterior_draws.

Fields:

  • names: the model parameters, as set_parameters! names them;
  • values: draws × names, on the natural scale;
  • sigma_b: the bulk observation noise of each draw, on the natural scale, or nothing when the posterior has none. It is not a cell parameter, and is never written to a model;
  • chain and iteration: where each draw sits in the run that produced it;
  • weights: normalised importance weights (an ABC posterior), or nothing for equally weighted draws;
  • truth: the dataset's true values by name on the natural scale, σb under `:sigmab, when the source records them; otherwisenothing`;
  • source: what the draws were read from. kind is one of :v7_replicate, :b7_chain, :chains, :abc or :merged, beside what the source records.

InferCell.posterior_draws — Method
posterior_draws(chain::Chains; names = nothing, truth = nothing,
                source = (kind = :chains,)) -> PosteriorDraws
posterior_draws(result::ABCPosterior) -> PosteriorDraws
posterior_draws(path::AbstractString; burn, thin) -> PosteriorDraws
posterior_draws(paths::AbstractVector{<:AbstractString}; kwargs...) -> PosteriorDraws

Read a posterior into a PosteriorDraws, mapping every column to its parameter by name.

A Chains reads its :parameters section and leaves the sampler's internals. A column log_<name> is <name> on the log scale, as infer(models, ds; route) writes it, and any other column is on the natural scale, as the toy models' NUTS path writes it. sigma_b, on either scale, becomes the sigma_b field. A chain whose columns are x[1], x[2], … (Turing's names for a vector parameter) needs names, the column names in index order. Every chain of the Chains is read, not only the first.

A file is deserialized and read by what it holds:

  • a V7 replicate file, (; state, meta), from dev/scripts/sbc_v7_16a9b.jl. Its draws are the sweeps after the first burn, every thin-th. The default burn = 100 is V7's. The sweeps come from one correlated chain (23 of the 50 replicates have a smallest bulk ESS under 100), so thin (default 1) is there to thin it. The names are meta.names, and the truth is meta.truth. burn and thin apply to V7 files only, and are refused for the others;
  • a B7 chain file, from dev/scripts/av6_89_b7.jl. Its x[i] columns are the mixed route's names in order, log_ and then M0_TARGETS plus σ_b. The truth comes from the fit's prep.jls beside it, when that file holds the dataset;
  • a Chains saved on its own.

A PosteriorDraws is returned as it is.

Several files are read in turn and merged, as a fit's two B7 chain files are. Their parameters must match, and so must their truths when they record one, their B7 fits, and their routes (V7 is the exact route's, B7 the mixed route's, and a Chains records its own). A file read twice is refused. If two parts would give a draw the same chain and iteration, as two infer outputs do (each is chain 1), the later part's chains are numbered after the earlier's.

InferCell.held_parameters — Method
held_parameters(models, inferred; driver = nothing) -> (; held, skipped)

The parameters a prediction completes from their priors: every free parameter of models that is not among inferred (names, or a PosteriorDraws). held is in composition order. Four kinds are skipped, and each is listed in skipped as (name, reason), so a prediction's provenance can say what it left out:

  • :inferred: the posterior has it, unless it is of the next two kinds;
  • :driver_written: the driver overwrites it at every handshake, so a value set there is never read. The enz_R_* catalytic slots are of this kind (driver_written_params);
  • :haldane_derived: a reverse constant, which haldane_values derives from the values that run;
  • :point_prior: a Dirac prior, with nothing to draw.

driver is the composition's built HandshakeDriver. If none is passed, one is built over a one-second span, with the global generator restored after.

InferCell.Knockdown — Type
Knockdown(; param = :S_JCVISYN3A_0779, dose)

A promoter knockdown: param is scaled by 1 − dose after the parameter draw, and nothing else changes, beyond a reverse constant derived from it (intervene). The default is the ptsG promoter, the first case (KIM-32). It is an inferred parameter, and its effect crosses the gene-expression–metabolism boundary. dose is in [0, 1], and the dose screen (KIM-37) picks it. A dose of 0 changes nothing, so its effect is exactly zero.

InferCell.intervene — Method
intervene(a, models, values) -> Vector{Pair{Symbol, Float64}}

The parameter writes values with intervention a applied. For a Knockdown, the parameter's drawn value is scaled by 1 − dose. If values does not set it, the scaling starts from its nominal value. The reverse constants are then derived from the scaled set (haldane_values), so a knockdown of a forward constant moves its reverse constant with it, and every intervened arm keeps the nominal equilibrium constants, as its baseline does.

InferCell.CellRun — Type
CellRun

One forward run, as predict keeps it: one hybrid cell, or one mean-field solve.

Fields:

  • seed: the cell's seed, or 0 for the mean field, which has none;
  • q: the predicted quantity, NaN when the run failed;
  • failure: :none, or why the run did not finish. It is :overdraw when the mean field overdrew a pool (its form of starving), :not_advanced when the hybrid's metabolic block stopped, and :retcode when a mean-field solve ended without success, such as at its step cap;
  • message: the failure's text, or empty;
  • drains: the drains the hybrid made, or 0 for the mean field;
  • clips: clip_summary's rows, one per counter that clipped. The mean field drains unclamped, so it never clips;
  • minima: each ODE state's smallest particle count over the run, the meters aside. It is read at every handshake for the hybrid, and only at the saves for the mean field;
  • accounts: carbon_accounts for the run, or nothing when it failed or is unmetered.

InferCell.DeltaCorrection — Type
DeltaCorrection

The mean field's correction for Q, computed once at a reference point and kept so it can be reused (delta_correction): δ = μ̂hybrid(θref) − m(θ_ref), added to the mean field's Q at every draw. It assumes δ is constant over the posterior's support, which the crossed design tests on M0 (dev/notes/2026-10-29-cut-predictive-distribution.md §5.4).

Fields:

  • values: θ_ref's parameter writes;
  • horizon and quantity: what Q is;
  • modules: the composition's module ids, which a prediction reusing this correction must match;
  • seeds and hybrid: the ensemble's cells;
  • mean_field: the mean field's run at θ_ref;
  • hybrid_mean, hybrid_se and n_ok: the mean of Q over the cells that finished, its standard error, and how many finished;
  • delta: hybrid_mean − mean_field.q;
  • intervention: the intervention the correction also covers, or nothing. The intervened arm runs on the same seeds, and hybrid_intervened, mean_field_intervened and delta_intervened are its counterparts, so the corrected mean-field effect is m(g_a θ) − m(θ) + delta_intervened − delta.

InferCell.delta_correction — Method
delta_correction(models, values, seeds; horizon, quantity = :lactate_out,
                 intervention = nothing) -> DeltaCorrection

The hybrid ensemble at the reference writes values, one cell per seed, against the mean field there. Reverse constants are derived from values (haldane_values). With an intervention, the intervened arm runs too, on the same seeds (intervene). A cell that fails is kept but left out of the mean. If fewer than two cells finish, or the mean field fails at θ_ref, δ is undefined and the call throws.

InferCell.forward_cells — Method
forward_cells(models, values, seeds; forward = :hybrid, horizon,

Run models at the parameter writes values (name => value pairs, written as given). Unlike intervention_cells and delta_correction, this derives no reverse constants. Pass values through haldane_values first to keep every equilibrium constant. The composition is metered first.

InferCell.ViabilityRule — Type
ViabilityRule(; label, allow_clipped, min_viable_fraction, starved_fraction = 0.5,
              floors = Pair{Symbol, Float64}[])

A declared viability rule V, passed to predict as data.

Each cell is put in one category, in this order of precedence (cell_status):

  1. :failed: the run did not finish;
  2. :starved: some counter clipped at more than starved_fraction of the drains. The default 0.5 is 14c's census rule. For the mean field, an overdrawn pool is its form of starving;
  3. :below_floor: some state in floors (species => particles) went below its floor at some point in the run;
  4. :clipped: some counter clipped at least once;
  5. :clean.

A cell is viable when it is :clean, or :clipped with allow_clipped.

V is a property of θ, not of a cell. θ is viable when at least min_viable_fraction of its cells are. A per-cell V would condition the cells rather than the parameters, and would change what the population mean means (note §3.3). The mean field has one run per θ, so the fraction there is 0 or 1. label names the rule in a prediction's provenance.

InferCell.viability_weights — Method
viability_weights(v::AbstractMatrix{Bool}) -> NamedTuple

The two conditioned variants of note §3.3 as weights on one crossed matrix, where v[m, k] is V's decision for posterior draw m beside held draw k. M3 does not pick between them; M4's freeze does.

  • per_theta_I, variant (b): w = v / (M′ · Σ_k v). Each θI row that keeps a viable θH weighs 1/M′, so θI's marginal stays the posterior's, conditional on at least one viable θH per row. M′ counts those rows.
  • joint, variant (a): w ∝ v. A θI row weighs its acceptance rate P̂(V | θI), so the filter acts on θ_I as data.

Also returned: the overall acceptance rate; row_acceptance, which is P̂(V | θI^m) per row; `failedrows, the θ_I rows with no viable θ_H, which (b) leaves out and reports; and the Kish effective sample size of each weight set,essperthetaIandessjoint`. With no viable cell anywhere, both weight sets are zero and both ESS are 0.
