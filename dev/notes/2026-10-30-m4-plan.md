## Commits, in order

### 1. KIM-62: parallel `predict` (threads)

Files: `src/predict.jl`, `src/predict_forward.jl`, `test/test_predict.jl`.

- **The keyword.** `predict(...; threads::Bool = Threads.nthreads() > 1)` follows
  the repo's pattern (`_each` in `src/csmc.jl:386`, `block1_replay`, `gibbs_sweep!`).
  `delta_correction` gets the same keyword.
- **The loop, in two parallel phases.**
  1. Every baseline run (M·K·n_cells hybrid cells, or M·K mean-field solves) runs
     through `_each` into preallocated slots.
  2. V's decisions are read serially.
  3. The intervened runs of the viable pairs run in a second parallel phase.

  δ's ensemble cells use the same runner. Seeds, the stream and the
  `_restoring_global_rng` wrapper do not change.
- **Why threads are safe:**
  - A hybrid cell is self-contained. It calls `Random.seed!` (task-local in
    Julia 1.10), then builds, sets its parameters and runs (`_hybrid_cell`).
  - The mean field shares one `MeanFieldRoute`. `solve_mean_field` copies `p`
    and calls `remake`, so the first step checks that `_RouteRHS` holds no
    shared mutable buffer. If it does, each task gets its own route.
- **Errors keep their types.** A `TaskFailedException` or `CompositeException`
  is unwrapped and its first underlying exception rethrown, so a refusal is
  still an `ArgumentError`.
- **Provenance** records the thread count, outside `settings`.
- **The test** is a threaded-equals-serial testset, run in the suite at 4
  threads, on what the file already compiles:
  - a d11 run at 120 s (M = 2, K = 2, n_cells = 2) under a toy V and a knockdown;
  - an M0 mean field with δ (n_ref = 4).

  It checks `mu`, `sigma2`, `n_ok`, `effect`, every cell's seed, q and failure,
  the decisions, the weights, both summaries, and the provenance pairs. It is
  about 30 cells, because the CI unit job is at 79–87 of 90 minutes (KIM-65).
- **The docstring and §12** say what to do at each scale. M0 (`completion = :none`)
  still splits over array tasks, with an rng each and disjoint `draws`. A
  crossed matrix runs threaded on one node.

### 3. KIM-E (new): the variance-split estimator on the crossed matrix, and the MC term

Files: `src/predict_variance.jl` (new), `src/predict.jl`, a test, and
`dev/scripts/variance_split_check_kimE.{jl,sh}`.

- **The MC term.** `_summary` uses Σ v(1−v)τ / (1−Σv²), KIM-63 item 1. It is
  exact for any weights.
- **`variance_split(p::Prediction; weights)`** gives:
  - the total;
  - the I-first order: Var_I[E_{H|I} μ] and E_I[Var_{H|I} μ];
  - the H-first order;
  - the gap between the orders;
  - the MC term removed;
  - a flag on each negative component, which is reported as zero;
  - the counts it used.
- **How it estimates:**
  - **Unmasked (`uniform`):** the balanced two-way random-effects method of
    moments, with τ = σ̂²/n known (MS_A, MS_B, MS_AB). It is unbiased.
  - **Masked (`per_theta_I`, `joint`):** within-row moments, and the spread of
    the row means corrected for within-row noise and for the covariance from
    shared columns. The H-first order is read through weights ∝ v/P̂(V | θ_I).
    The exact form is fixed by the check below.
- **The simulation check** is a dev script run via Slurm, plus a small unit test.
  - μ = f(θ_I) + g(θ_H) + h(θ_I, θ_H), with known parts and known noise τ.
  - Three masks: none, independent Bernoulli, and dependent on θ. The true split
    under each conditioned law is computed by brute force.
  - It runs at the planned M and K (from KIM-38) and at smaller ones, with 2,000
    replicates, plus 200k replicates for the MC term at v = (.7, .1, .1, .1).
  - **Pass:** every component's bias is within ±10%.
  - If H-first fails under the θ-dependent mask, the freeze reports I-first
    only, as note §5.2's fallback allows.

**Then: push, and run the suite** via `sbatch test/run_tests.slurm` at that
commit. The pilot commits below are pushed with it.

### 4–6. The pilot scripts: KIM-38, KIM-35, KIM-37 (each its own commit)

Each commit holds a `dev/scripts/<name>.jl`, a launcher `.sh` and nothing else.
Each script states its decision rule in its header before it runs. The rules
below become declared once this plan is approved.

**KIM-38 (`cost_pilot_kim38`): what a cell costs.**
- θ comes from v6's posterior, with θ_H from the prior. It times warm cells:
  - M0 hybrid, 600 s, baseline and knockdown, 20 cells each;
  - M0 mean field, 10 solves;
  - d11 hybrid at 6,300 s and at 600 s, 10 cells each;
  - d11 mean field at 6,300 s, 5 solves;
  - threaded throughput of a small d11 matrix at 16 and 64 threads, for
    efficiency and MaxRSS (by `sacct`).
- **The sizing run** is an M0 `predict` on v6 (20 draws × 10 cells, both forwards).
  It gives Var_θ[μ] and σ² for the sizing rules.

**KIM-38: the projection.** It counts the package's cells and multiplies by each
cell's cost.

| Part of the package | Proposed size |
|---|---|
| M0 crossed comparison | 50 datasets × 2 posteriors |
| Hybrid forward | 100 draws × n_cells |
| Mean field | 100 solves, plus a δ ensemble of n_ref per dataset × posterior |
| The truth μ(θ*) | n_truth cells per dataset |
| Intervention | doubles the hybrid, δ and truth cells |
| Reduced-model budget | 100 × K × n_cells, plus V's own cells if chosen, at 3 widths |

- **The sizing rules** (declared here):
  - the MC term is at most 10% of the spread over draws;
  - the truth's SE is at most 10% of the predictive SD;
  - δ's SE is at most 10% of the predictive SD.
- **Wall-clock** = CPU-h divided by a stated concurrency (from B7's concurrent av6
  jobs), with a margin for the queue, set against Nov 20.
- **The gate**, as in the issue:
  1. if the run passes Nov 20, cut the intervention panel;
  2. if it still does, run 2 widths, not 3.

  Each cut goes into §12.

**KIM-35 (`sensitivity_pilot_kim35`), on `d11_models`:**
- **Arm I:** θ_I is 50 v6 posterior draws, and θ_H stays nominal (`completion = :none`).
- **Arm H:** θ_I is fixed at v6's posterior mean (a one-draw `PosteriorDraws`),
  and θ_H is 50 prior draws (`completion = :prior`, M = 1).
- Each arm runs n_cells = 4, threaded, at 6,300 s and at 600 s. Both Q
  candidates come from the same cells (`CellRun.accounts`: `lactate_out` and
  `exported`).
- **Also:** mean-field local sensitivities, ±0.2 in ln θ, for each of the 20
  parameters at both horizons. And the unfiltered `CELL_STATUSES` counts, so V's
  thresholds see held-out acceptance rates.
- **Rule:** a (Q, horizon) passes if each group's parameter variance, net of MC:
  - is above 2 bootstrap SEs; and
  - is at least 5% of Var_I + Var_H.

  If no candidate passes at the chosen horizon, the note recommends a change and
  M4 stops for you.
- **Cost:** about 4.5 CPU-h.

**KIM-37 (`dose_screen_kim37`): which dose stays in the validated regime.**
- **Draws:**
  - on M0 at 600 s: 50 fresh prior θ_I draws (`draw_parameters(rng, m0_models(),
    M0_TARGETS)`), 4 cells each;
  - on d11 at the reduced horizon: 20 draws × 2 cells. This is because the O2
    claim includes intervention predictions on the reduced model (see the gate).
- **Doses** 0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 and 0.5, with seeds paired across doses.
- **How it measures.** The script steps the driver itself, as `_hybrid_cell`
  does, 60 handshakes at a time. At every rebuild instant it records:
  - ATP, GTP and `M_trna_chg_c`, in mM;
  - `rate_constant_elasticity(d)` for those pools;
  - a pool at zero, counted as its own category.

  It does not watch CTP or UTP, which are chemostats. **It computes no ΔQ.**
- **The criterion** concerns validity only. A dose is admissible when both hold
  for each watched pool:
  1. the 95th percentile, over draws × cells × instants, of the pool's largest
     elasticity is at most max(0.1, 1.1 × its dose-0 value);
  2. the fraction of instants with the pool below its K_d (0.1 mM for ATP and
     GTP, 0.02 mM lumped for charged tRNA) is at most dose 0's fraction plus 0.01.
- **Selection:** the largest admissible dose in {0.1, 0.2, 0.3}. Doses 0.4 and
  0.5 only show where the regime ends. If no dose up to 0.3 is admissible, the
  panel is dropped (the cut line), and §12 records it.
- **Cost:** about 3 CPU-h.
