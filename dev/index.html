InferCell.jl

Composable, inference-first whole-cell modelling in Julia.

Whole-cell models mix formalisms. Metabolism is a system of ordinary differential equations, gene expression is a stochastic jump process, and the two exchange state on a clock. InferCell asks whether parameter uncertainty can be propagated correctly across that seam: when the model is fitted to data, do the posteriors mean what they claim to mean?

It answers on a reduced JCVI-syn3A model, cut from the published syn3A whole-cell model of Thornburg et al. (2022). The model is built from modules that touch only through a typed coupling boundary, and fitted by a sampler that treats the stochastic path as a latent variable.

What's here

  • The reduced syn3A model. Twenty-two metabolic reactions in four ODE modules and seventeen genes in three jump modules, which swap state every simulated second and rate constants every sixty.
  • A composition framework. Each module declares the states it owns and its coupling edges against one interface contract, and the resolver checks the composition before anything runs. Parameters carry their upstream provenance, and every departure from the published model is enumerated.
  • The hybrid driver. It steps the ODE and jump blocks in lock-step, reports conservation, clipping and growth from the outside, and replays a recorded jump path exactly.
  • A conditional sampler. A three-block Gibbs chain over the ODE parameters, the stochastic rate constants and the jump path. The path is updated by conditional SMC with exact transcript bridges.
  • Synthetic data. Observables, noise models and a dataset generator that records its truth, seeds and reductions.
  • Toy models. The ODE and stochastic models the package started from, with a single infer() call.

Status

Pre-release research code.

  • Done and tested: the model, the framework, the hybrid driver and each sampler block.
  • Running: the long reference chain and the simulation-based calibration of the sampler on a two-gene reference system. Calibration has not yet passed, and this release makes no claim that the posteriors are calibrated.
  • Not started: recovery and coverage on the full reduced model.
  • Not in scope: real data. The reduced model does not reproduce syn3A's doubling time, metabolite levels or proteome, and is not to be compared with them.
