The reduced syn3A model

A small organism cut from the published JCVI-syn3A whole-cell model of Thornburg et al. (2022). It keeps what makes a whole-cell model hard to infer on: a genuine split between deterministic metabolism and stochastic gene expression, coupling in both directions, low-copy discrete species, and autocatalytic closure. About 92% of the cell is removed. What is left is the best-measured region of the network.

In the code, the CoreA and corea prefixes name this model: build_corea, corea_models, src/organisms/coreA/.

What it contains

ODE block22 reactions in 4 modules: glycolysis through lactate (10), the phosphotransferase cascade plus lactate export (6), nucleotide recycling (5), and a lumped tRNA charging step (1)
Stochastic block52 reactions in 3 modules: transcription, translation and transcript decay for 17 genes, plus one translocation for ptsG, the only membrane protein
State32 dynamic species and 5 chemostats, named and ordered once in a registry
Copy numbersmRNA 0–2, protein 266–1,355, metabolites about 200–74,000 particles
Coupling3 channels exchanged every 1 s, 2 every 60 s, and a volume channel in both directions
GrowthSurface-area accounting. The cell reaches about 1.07× its initial volume in one 6,300 s cycle

The seven modules are CentralGlycolysis, PtsTransport, NucleotideRecycling and TrnaCharging in the ODE block, and CoreATranscription, CoreATranslation and CoreATranscriptDecay in the jump block. corea_models returns them as the assembled model composes them.

How the blocks talk

Through state, not through parameters. No rate constant appears in both blocks. Enzyme copy numbers from translation set metabolic rates. Nucleotide and charged-tRNA pools from metabolism set transcription and translation rates. This is a property of the published model, not of the reduction, and it shapes the sampler: there is no shared parameter whose posterior could be passed across the seam, so information crosses through the path.

Each module declares the states it owns and its typed coupling edges. The resolver checks the composition before it runs: every state has an owner, every edge a partner, and no moiety is stranded. See the interface contract.

The handshake. Every simulated second, counts from the jump block become rate-law parameters of the ODE block, the ODE block integrates one second, and the costs the jump block accrued are debited from the metabolite pools. Every sixty seconds, the jump block's rate constants are rebuilt from those pools. The handshake driver runs this, and records it from the outside so conservation and clipping can be checked.

Two variants of one model

  • The published model, build_corea(), clamps a drained pool at zero and carries the shortfall, and writes pools back as whole particles. It generates every synthetic dataset.
  • The smoothed model, build_corea(; COREA_SAMPLER...), smooths the clamp over one particle and carries pools continuously. It is differentiable through the handshake, and kept for gradient-based work. The conditional sampler does not need it: its parameter block is gradient-free and runs on the published model.

What it is not

It does not reproduce syn3A's 105-minute doubling time, its metabolite concentrations or its proteome, and no result is to be compared with them. doubling_time throws rather than return a number; growth is reported as fractional growth or time to a threshold (growth_report).

Several treatments are ours and not the published model's. These include the lumped charging step, the chemostatted CTP, UTP and amino-acid pools, and exogenous membrane growth. reduction_report enumerates each, so the label can reach any result that depends on one.

M0, the two-gene reference system

The sampler is validated on M0, the same model cut to two genes over 600 s: GAPD (JCVISYN3A_0607) and ptsG (JCVISYN3A_0779). The other enzyme counts are held at their proteomics copy numbers (HeldEnzymeCounts), so the catalytic channel runs as it does in the full model with one live count.

M0 has five free parameters (M0_TARGETS): the two promoter strengths, the transcript decay constant, and the enolase and aldolase forward constants. The sixth is the metabolite noise scale. m0_dataset draws a truth from the prior and simulates a population of cells under it. Metabolites are observed as one bulk measurement per pool and time, and transcripts exactly per cell.
