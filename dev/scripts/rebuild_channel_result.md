## 4.5 — the elasticity of a rebuilt rate constant to its pool

`ToyRebuiltExpression` rebuilds `k_tx_rb = k_max · atp / (km + atp)`, whose
elasticity `d ln k / d ln atp` is `km / (km + atp)` in closed form. The
diagnostic is a central difference in log space, so the analytic column is a
check on the measurement rather than a restatement of it.

Every row agrees with the closed form to better than 1e-5 relative.

**The gain is a function of the pool, not a constant of the model**, which is
what spec §10 R8 requires of every channel gain: the same rebuild law reports an
elasticity spanning more than an order of magnitude across the pool range above.
The toy's law is ours and its `km` is arbitrary; the number that matters is the
*real* transcription channel's, which `dev/notes/reduced-syn3a-scoping.md`
measures at 0.044–0.051 and which phase 10 computes from the published rate law.
What this table establishes is that the diagnostic recovers a known elasticity
exactly, so the phase-10 number will be the model's and not the estimator's.

## 4.6 — spec §4 D10's granularity comparison, in miniature

300 replicates per granularity, 600 s horizon. Differences are **paired per
seed** against the published 1 s drain and sampled at **drain-aligned**
handshakes (every 60 s), where every configuration has just debited the same
total accrual — so what is left is the difference in the dynamics and not the
unpaid balance. One row per pool, nothing selected: `final` is the difference
at the last aligned instant, and `max` is the largest over that pool's own
instants with `n` the multiplicity it was selected out of.

| drain (s) | pool | final | ± SE | max | ± SE | at t (s) | n | resolved? | vs D10's 1% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | M_atp_c | +0.00019 | 0.00021 | +0.00019 | 0.00021 | 600 | 10 | no | below 1% |
| 5 | M_adp_c | -0.00168 | 0.00164 | -0.00236 | 0.00131 | 300 | 10 | no | below 1% |
| 60 | M_atp_c | +0.00006 | 0.00021 | +0.00011 | 0.00004 | 60 | 10 | no | below 1% |
| 60 | M_adp_c | -0.00041 | 0.00168 | -0.00124 | 0.00087 | 180 | 10 | no | below 1% |

And the quantity that is **not** a granularity cost, reported separately so it
cannot be mistaken for one: the sawtooth of the outstanding debit, measured on
the debited pool one handshake before the last drain, beside its closed form.

| drain (s) | pool | measured | ± SE | closed form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | M_atp_c | +0.00062 | 0.00021 | +0.00044 |
| 60 | M_atp_c | +0.00648 | 0.00021 | +0.00644 |

The closed-form column is exactly that: `(drain − interval) · k_tl · (k_tx/γ) ·
cost / (factor · pool)`, computed from the toy's own declared parameters with no
simulation entering it at all. It is what an unaligned sampling instant
measures, and a first version of this script reported it as the granularity
cost. It is bookkeeping — the cost has been accrued and not yet paid, not a
trajectory that went somewhere else.

The driver's own policy, as `reduction_report(models, driver)` renders it for
the 60 s configuration:

    1 declaration(s) that are this reduction's, not the published model's:
      coarse_drain:
        - the deferred counters are debited every 60.0 s rather than at every 1.0 s handshake, so the pools see one aggregate drain where the published model applies 60 — a labelled reduction whose cost spec §4 D10 requires to be measured, not assumed

**Read narrowly.** This is the toy, not Core A′. Its ATP pool is ~404,000
particles against a drain of order 40 particles per second, a buffer of hours,
while the pools D10's comparison is really about turn over in 109 s (adenylate)
and 30 s (guanylate) — which is why D10 expects 60 s to fail there and 5 s to
pass. A small difference here bounds the *mechanism* — that the debit
aggregates, clamps and pays correctly — and nothing else. The measurement that
decides the granularity is phase 13's, on the assembled model; what this phase
delivers is the knob, the labelling and the method: drain-aligned sampling,
paired per-seed differences, the multiplicity of any maximum stated, and the
sawtooth reported apart from the dynamics.
