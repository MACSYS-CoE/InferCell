### Conservation, against the round-off floor

| moiety | residual at (1e-10, 1e-8) | at a tenth of each | ratio | integrator bound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| adenylate | 3.491e-13 mM | 1.208e-13 mM | 2.9x | 4.172e-08 mM |
| guanylate | 1.044e-14 mM | 1.354e-14 mM | 0.8x | 2.034e-08 mM |

**Tightening the solver does not shrink these residuals, and it should not.**
Both moieties are linear invariants, and a Runge-Kutta or Rosenbrock method
preserves a linear invariant exactly — with `nᵀJ = 0` the vector passes
through the `(I − γhJ)⁻¹` solve unchanged, so every stage increment sums to
zero. There is no integration error in this quantity to scale. What the
table shows is round-off: five orders of magnitude below the integrator
bound in the last column, and slightly *larger* at the tighter setting
because tightening adds steps. Spec §3's five-fold rule binds where the
trajectory is restarted — the assembled model's 6,300 handshakes, which is
what the bound's `N_restarts` factor is for — and not here (§12, amended
2026-09-10). The evidence that these checks can fail is the mutation test in
`test/test_corea_nucleotide_recycling.jl`, not this table.

### Pyrophosphatase removed

Pyrophosphate reaches **12.9074 mM** at 6,300 s from 0.1000 mM — a 129-fold
rise, against **0.370593 mM** in the configuration that keeps the enzyme.

**It strands the moiety; it does not diverge, and it cannot.** Phosphate is
closed in this composition at 35.0920 mM, and pyrophosphate ends holding
**73.6%** of it. With no route back to free phosphate the
substrate-level phosphorylation has nothing to work with, so ATP collapses
— below 1% of its initial value at t = 460.0 s, and 4.684e-18 mM at the end —
and charging stops with it, which is why the rise flattens (0.0000 mM
over the last save interval) rather than continuing.

The scoping note's **173 mM** is one pyrophosphate per charging event over a
whole cycle with nothing hydrolysing it. That is open-pool arithmetic: it
assumes charging runs at 553.1/s for 6,300 s, which needs a phosphate supply
the closed model does not have — Core A′'s own phosphate is a closed moiety,
which is what spec §3 check 4b asserts. Both numbers are recorded with the
model each belongs to. A stalled pathway is the stronger demonstration that
the enzyme is required, and it is the one that survives assembly.

## 8.7 — phosphate closure, both forms

| phosphate (flux-corrected) | 7.887e-13 mM | 3.837e-13 mM | 2.1x |

Phosphate crossing the two inbound mass edges over the cycle: 0.0505 mM,
carried in from the held 13DPG and PEP pools, one per turnover of the GTP
branch. It is bounded because guanylate is: once GDP has been driven to GTP
the branch stops. The flux-corrected form subtracts it; widening the bound to
swallow it instead would have hidden any real error of the same size.
The substrate-level phosphorylation crosses nothing — it turned over 345.13 mM
of phosphate over the cycle and took every one of them from the free pool,
which is what keeps the moiety closed and what a first version of the double
got wrong.

Both forms are linear invariants, so the ratio column carries the same
meaning it does above: tolerance-independence, not a five-fold fall.

## Wall-clock

One 6,300 s trajectory: **0.003 s** at the pinned tolerances, 0.005 s at
a tenth of each. Thirteen states and five reactions, no handshake — this is
not Core A′'s cost and does not decide K1, which spec task 13.7 measures on
the assembled hybrid model.

It does answer §9's question of whether the full-cycle checks belong in the
default suite, and the answer is that the *horizon* is free: at a few
milliseconds a trajectory, running 6,300 s rather than 600 s costs nothing,
so `test/test_corea_nucleotide_recycling.jl` asserts the phase's done-when at
the horizon the done-when names. What is not free is compilation — the two
full-cycle testsets add about 54 s to the suite, almost all of it Rodas5P
specialising on two new problem types, and shortening the horizon would not
recover any of it. This driver is not a duplicate of those tests: it is where
the numbers are *recorded*, with the commit and job that produced them, so a
reader gets the measurements without running anything.

## What this does not cover

Every number here is the module's against *doubles*. `HeldGlycolytic` holds
four glycolytic pools fixed and rephosphorylates ADP at a rate matched to the
charging demand; phase 6 replaces it with reactions that do neither exactly.
`ChargingDrain` is zeroth order in the tRNA pools where phase 9's module is
mass-action in both. The conservation and closure results are structural and
survive that replacement; the settled pyrophosphate concentration and the
crossing time do not, and are quoted as this composition's.
