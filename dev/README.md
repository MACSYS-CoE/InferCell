# dev/

Development and research material for InferCell — **not** part of the published
documentation. Everything here is working material from building InferCell and
may be dropped or rewritten before release. The published, user-facing docs live
in [`../docs/`](../docs/) (rendered with MkDocs).

- `plans/` — design and decision log (vision & v0.0.1 scope, boundary
  representation, architecture overview, historical phase-1 design).
- `notes/` — research notes (the case for inference, calibration curve,
  background reading on the 4D / spatial minimal cell).
- `talks/` — slide decks and talk material.
- `log/` — dated implementation logs.
- `council/`, `references/` — local-only (git-ignored): council transcripts and
  large reference PDFs.
