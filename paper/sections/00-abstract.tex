\begin{abstract}
Whole-cell models simulate a living cell from its molecular parts. They usually fix each
parameter at one value, so a prediction does not say how much of it the data support.
Inference is hard because the models mix deterministic and stochastic formalisms, the
stochastic paths are latent, and no parameter is shared between the two. We present
\IC{}, a compositional inference framework. Parameters carry priors and sources, and the framework checks each composition and lists its declared reductions. \IC{} supports exact stochastic and
mean-field descriptions across a coupled ODE--jump boundary, with two inference routes:
an exact sampler, and a cheaper mixed route that integrates out the stochastic paths. We demonstrate it on a reduced model of the minimal cell \syn{} and on M0, a two-gene slice of that model. On M0, both routes pass simulation-based calibration over
\pn{vseven.R} datasets, with small declared discrepancies. The mixed route costs a median of
\pn{mix.cpuh} CPU-hours per posterior, against \pn{ften.cpuh}--\pn{cost.exacthi}. On the one
dataset with a long exact reference, three parameters sit
\pn{mix.gaplo}--\pn{mix.gaphi} standard deviations from it, and the cause is open. Posterior-to-prediction
scoring on M0 covers lactate export at the expected rate, a quantity the data measure noisily through extracellular lactate. A knockdown's effect, which the data do not measure, has the right
sign, though those intervals are not a clean calibration. We then carry an M0 posterior into the reduced model, with viability-conditioned
uncertainty in the remaining parameters, to make forward and intervention predictions.
These predictions are unscored. At the default prior width, among the \pn{budget.ndrawn}
parameters drawn and on one posterior, about \pn{budget.heldpct}\%~$\pm$~\pn{budget.heldpctse}\%
of the parameter uncertainty in lactate export comes from \pn{budget.nH} promoter strengths
that the fit cannot reach, and that share runs from \pn{budget.heldpctlo}\% to
\pn{budget.heldpcthi}\% as their asserted prior width changes. The data are synthetic. The
framework shows what a prediction rests on. We claim no all-parameter posterior, no
calibrated inference on the reduced model, and no biological finding.
\end{abstract}
