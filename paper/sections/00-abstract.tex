\begin{abstract}
Whole-cell models simulate a living cell from its molecular parts. They fix every
parameter to one value, so each prediction is a single sample with no error bar.
The models cannot say how much of a prediction the data support. Inference is hard
because the models mix deterministic and stochastic formalisms, the stochastic paths
are latent, and no parameter is shared across the boundary between the two blocks.
We present \IC{}, a compositional inference framework for whole-cell modelling,
written in Julia and demonstrated on a reduced model of the minimal cell \syn{}. Every
parameter is a prior distribution with a recorded source. Modules couple through typed
edges, and the framework checks the composition and lists every reduction from the
published model. \IC{} supports exact stochastic and mean-field descriptions across a
coupled ODE--jump boundary. On M0, a two-gene slice of the model, both inference routes
pass simulation-based calibration over \pn{vseven.R} datasets, with small declared
discrepancies. The mixed route costs a median of \pn{mix.cpuh} CPU-hours per posterior,
against \pn{ften.cpuh}--\pn{cost.exacthi} for the exact sampler. Its largest mean bias
against the exact posteriors is \pn{mix.biasmax}~$\pm$~\pn{mix.biasse} standard
deviations, and on one dataset three parameters sit \pn{mix.gaplo}--\pn{mix.gaphi}
standard deviations from the exact reference, for a reason still open. We then combine
an M0 posterior modularly with viability-conditioned uncertainty in the remaining
parameters, to make forward and intervention predictions on the reduced model. These
predictions are unscored. At the default prior width, among the \pn{budget.ndrawn}
parameters drawn and on one posterior, about \pn{budget.heldpct}\% of the parameter
uncertainty in lactate export comes from \pn{budget.nH} promoter strengths that the fit
cannot reach, and that share runs from \pn{budget.heldpctlo}\% to \pn{budget.heldpcthi}\%
as their asserted prior width changes. Scored on M0, the same pipeline covers lactate
export at the expected rate, and it gets the sign and scale of a knockdown's effect right.
The data are synthetic and the priors asserted. We claim no all-parameter posterior, no
calibrated inference on the reduced model, and no biological finding.
\end{abstract}
