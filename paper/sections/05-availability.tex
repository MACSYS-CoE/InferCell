\section*{Software and data availability}
\label{sec:availability}

\IC{} is open source at \url{https://github.com/MACSYS-CoE/InferCell}, with
documentation at \url{https://macsys-coe.github.io/InferCell/}. The version used in this paper, and the evidence archive behind
its predictions, are deposited with the v0.3.0 release at \placeholder{Zenodo DOI, from the
release}. The archive holds the prediction runs' outputs and the posterior fits they read, with SHA-256 checksums. The repository holds the rank tables and chain summaries behind the calibration figures, in \path{dev/scripts/} and \path{paper/figures/data/}. Each number in this paper is listed with its source file in
\path{paper/numbers.tex}, and each run's result note in \path{dev/scripts/} gives its
commit and Slurm jobs. All data are synthetic.
