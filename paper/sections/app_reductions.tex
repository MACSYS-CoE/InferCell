\section{Declared reductions}
\label{app:reductions}

\subsection{Model form: what the budgets leave out}
\label{sec:modelform}

In \IC{} each departure from the published \syn{} model is a declared reduction. A
declared reduction is an object with a category and a reason, collected from the edges,
the parameter records and the modules (\cref{sec:design}). This appendix lists the
declared reductions of the reduced model and of M0. For each one it says what we know of
its effect on $Q$, the extracellular lactate of \cref{sec:predictapi}. On M0 the same quantity
is read at the end of M0's shorter horizon (\cref{sec:predm0}).

A prediction's uncertainty has four sources: inferred parameters, held parameters,
intrinsic noise and model form. The first three have numbers, on the reduced model's joint
posterior in \cref{sec:reducedpredict} and in the budget transferred from M0 in
\cref{sec:budget}. Model form has none. \Cref{tab:modelform} marks each reduction
by what we know of its effect on $Q$. Only the mean-field swap is measured, because only it
has a column in M0's crossed design (\cref{app:crossed}). Every other reduction is declared
and not bounded. So neither budget covers the full uncertainty a modeller should carry.
The true simulator of \cref{sec:reducedpredict} is the exact hybrid of the same reduced
model. It shares the model's own reductions, so the scores there do not test them.

\paragraph{The one-way reduction on the reduced model.} The elasticities that justify this
reduction hold while GTP stays well above $K_d$, the dissociation constant of RNA
polymerase for its nucleotides (\cref{sec:info}). In the run of the transferred budget, at the
default width, \pn{kd.lo}--\pn{kd.hi}\% of baseline cells fall below $K_d$ at least once, and
\pn{kd.zero}\% reach exactly zero. Clip counts give a lower limit on the time spent below $K_d$:
\pn{kd.boundn} of \pn{budget.ncellsbase} cells spend more than \pn{kd.boundpct}\% of their
post-debit instants at zero. This bound misses the time between zero and $K_d$, so it
undercounts. An earlier screen drew the inferred parameters from their priors rather than
from this posterior. It found GTP below $K_d$ at
\pn{kd.screenlo}--\pn{kd.screenhi}\% of rebuild instants in \pn{kd.screenn} of 20 draws,
and at none in \pn{kd.screenzero}. On those draws the basis does not hold over the full
cycle, whatever the dose. On M0 the same screen found GTP below $K_d$ at no
instant at the chosen dose, so the M0 check does not cover this caveat. In the scored
predictions of \cref{sec:reducedpredict}, \pn{kim95.clip}\% of baseline cells clip, at the truth
and under the joint posterior alike.
