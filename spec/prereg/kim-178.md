## 1. The question and the estimand

Is the promoters' downward offset one dataset's noise, or a property of the fitted
likelihood, the transcript factor in particular? The full cell inherits that factor.

For each dataset r and each promoter j (the 17 coordinates `log_S_*` of
`mixed_parameter_names(s)`):

    z_rj = (x̂_rj − x*_rj) / σ̂_rj

- x*_r is the truth in x, which is `ds.truth` as (log θ, log σ_b).
- x̂_r is the MAP of the fitted likelihood (§2).
- σ̂_rj = √[(−H_r)⁻¹]_jj, the Laplace SD at x̂_r.

The **replicate statistic** is m_r = mean of z_rj over the 17 promoters.

## 2. The design, per dataset

The fit is KIM-92's (`prereg/kim-92` §1), with only the seed changed. Then there is one
more optimisation and a Hessian.

1. **Dataset.**
   `corea_dataset(seed; ncells = 200, horizon = COREA_CYCLE_S, fit_to = COREA_FIT_S, targets = COREA_TARGETS)`.
   The seed's `Xoshiro` draws the truth from the prior (`corea_draws`). The fit window
   holds 884 bulk rows.
2. **Setup.** `mixed_setup(ds; models = d11_models(), targets = COREA_TARGETS)`, at its
   default tolerances and the ±6 prior SD support box.
3. **θ_ref.** `reference_theta(s; maxiters = 200)` at δ = S = 0, with its default reltol
   (1e-10) and xtol (1e-6).
4. **δ and S.** Run the reference ensemble of 800 cells, `reference_seeds(ds, 800)`,
   with `reference_ensemble(mixed_theta_values(s, θ_ref.x), seeds; models = d11_models(), horizon = ds.times[end])`.
   Then `delta_and_S(cells, m_ref; C = 200)`, with m_ref the mean-field log bulk at
   θ_ref.
5. **The fitted-likelihood MAP.** x̂ = `reference_theta(with_reference(s, δ, S); x0 = θ_ref.x, maxiters = 200)`,
   with the default reltol and xtol.
6. **Laplace.** −H at x̂ on the fitted density, by central differences of the
   ForwardDiff gradient at a step of 1e-3 × each coordinate's prior SD, symmetrised. This
   is `hessian_fd` of `dev/scripts/corea_pilot_kim92.jl`. σ̂ = √diag((−H)⁻¹), with no
   eigenvalue floor.

**KIM-92's own dataset** (seed 17_200_001) runs steps 5 and 6 only. It uses the archived
dataset, θ_ref, δ and S, read from `infercell-kim92-archive-2026-11-17` (`dataset.jls`,
`prep.jls`). It is reported beside the ten. It is **not** pooled into gate 2, because it is
the dataset that raised the question.

## 3. Seeds, failures and spares

- **The ten:** replicate seeds 17_200_002 to 17_200_011. They sit in the pilot injection
  block that `test/test_corea_mixed.jl` reserves (17_200_000 + r, r = 1…200). No result
  has used any of them, and 17_200_002 appears only in a 2-cell unit test. The reference
  seeds are derived from each replicate seed by `reference_seeds`.
- **Spares, in order:** 17_200_012 to 17_200_016.
- **A fit fails** if any of these holds:
  - either MAP (step 3 or step 5) returns `method = :prior_median`. That covers an
    optimiser error, a stop at maxiters without convergence, and a non-finite density.
  - any coordinate of x̂ lies within 0.01 prior SD of the support box.
  - −H at x̂ is not positive definite, or any σ̂_j is non-finite.
- **On a failure,** the dataset is reported with its reason, and the next spare replaces
  it. This repeats until ten datasets have succeeded, or the spares run out. A failed
  dataset's z values are never read into gate 2.
- **If the spares run out** before ten succeed, gate 2 is not read. That is a stop, and it
  goes to Tom.

## 4. Gate 2 (frozen; Tom, 2026-11-27)

Over the ten successful replicates, the transcript factor is called **miscalibrated** if
either of these holds:
- (a) m_r < 0 in at least 9 of the 10; or
- (b) |t| > 3, where t = m̄ / (s_m / √10). Here m̄ is the mean of the ten m_r, and s_m is
  their sample SD (n − 1). This is a t-statistic on 9 df, and the test is two-sided.

Otherwise the factor **passes** gate 2.

A fail means the factor needs a correction before the full cell. It also stops the work
and opens a discussion with Tom (project description, Gates). It is not a fallback.

Under calibration, (a) alone has a false-alarm rate of 11/1024 ≈ 1.1%. This is noted and
not adjusted.

## 5. The check on KIM-92's dataset, before the ten

Steps 5 and 6 run first on KIM-92's dataset, as one job. The work then stops for Tom's go
before the ten are submitted. This order is Tom's call. It is not a gate, and it does
not change §4.

That job reports:
- **The MAP check.** The fitted log density at x̂ and at θ_ref. The issue's check is that
  log p̃(x̂) ≥ log p̃(θ_ref).
- **Laplace against NUTS.** Per coordinate, σ̂_j at x̂ divided by the SD of KIM-92's 1,600
  pooled NUTS draws (`pilot_draws.tsv`), beside the 0.960 to 1.083 at θ_ref.
- **The promoters' mean z.** m by MAP and Laplace, against the NUTS posterior's −0.81.

## 6. Reported, not gated

- For each replicate:
  - z for all 21 coordinates;
  - m_r by θ_ref and its Laplace SD, beside m_r by x̂, which shows what δ and S move;
  - the number of the 17 promoters with z < 0;
  - the eigenvalues of −H, and its asymmetry before symmetrising;
  - the optimiser's method and wall time for each stage.
- The correlation-aware SE of m_r from (−H_r)⁻¹ (KIM-161's method), as a cross-check on
  (b).
- The cost: CPU-h per stage and in total, with the Slurm job ids.

There are ten truths drawn from the prior, so these are recovery statistics for this
design. They are not an SBC.
