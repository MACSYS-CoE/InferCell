# KIM-116 pre-registration: the modular arm's estimand, design, gates and comparison

Frozen at tag `prereg/kim-116` on 2026-11-19, before any modular posterior exists.
This is a snapshot of the criteria, not a copy of the plan. The plan lives in Linear
(project InferCell, issue KIM-116; Spec D7; Decision log 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-19).
A change to anything below after this tag is a Decision log entry that quotes Was and
Now, and says what had been read by then.

**What had been read when this was written.** The joint posterior on the same dataset
had already been read (KIM-92, `prereg/kim-92`), and so had its scored predictions
(KIM-95) and its uncertainty budget (KIM-96). That includes:
- the promoter strengths sitting low (14 of 17 standardised errors negative, mean −0.81);
- contraction ≥ 0.919 on all 21 coordinates.

Item 2's profile (Slurm 21196497) had also been read: it measured costs, plus the
plug-in's rates against route (ii)'s at the truth.

No modular draw had been read. Tom made the design calls below on 2026-11-19, in the
KIM-116 session, after the profile.

## 1. The estimand

The **cut distribution** of the reduced model on the pilot's dataset (Spec D7). This is
a separate estimand from the joint posterior.

Write φ for the 18 stage-1 coordinates: `log_<promoter>` for the 17 promoters, and
`log_krnadeg`. Write ψ for the 3 stage-2 coordinates: `log_kcatF_R_ENO`,
`log_kcatF_R_FBA` and `log_sigma_b`.

- **Stage 1** gives p₁(φ | transcripts). How depends on the variant (§2).
- **Stage 2** gives p₂(ψ | bulk, φ), the bulk factor alone with φ fixed:
  `modular_stage(bulk-only setup, ψ, x)`, with prior ψ's own.
- **The cut** is ∫ p₂(ψ | bulk, φ) p₁(φ | transcripts) dφ. Stage 2 does not feed back
  into φ.

## 2. Two variants of stage 1 (Decision log 2026-11-19)

1. **Cut, 1(b).**
   - The density: the transcript factor alone, `mixed_setup(…; streams = (:tx,))`,
     over all 21 coordinates. ENO, FBA and σ_b sit under their priors, and route (ii)
     is solved at each x.
   - What is kept: φ's marginal is stage 1's output, and its ψ draws are discarded.
2. **Plug-in, 2.**
   - The density: `plugin_loglik` over φ alone (`modular_stage(…; loglik = plugin)`,
     with ψ fixed at θ_ref's values, which do not move it).
   - The rates are rebuilt from `panel_plugin_states(s, θ_ref.x)`. That is the observed
     panel's `exp(log z − δ)` over the volume factor at θ_ref. Save 60m gives the value
     at switch 60m − 1, and t = 0 is the fixed initial condition.
   - CTP and UTP are chemostats.

## 3. The design

- **Dataset, setup, δ, S and θ_ref** are KIM-92's, read from
  `infercell-kim92-archive-2026-11-17`: `dataset.jls` and `prep.jls` (δ, S and
  `ref.x`). The setups are `mixed_setup(ds; models = d11_models(), targets =
  COREA_TARGETS, streams)` at their default tolerances, `with_reference(·, δ, S)`.
- **Stage 1, each variant: KIM-92's chain design** (prereg/kim-92 §1; Decision log
  2026-11-12).
  - NUTS(200, 0.8), maximum tree depth 10, 4 chains of 200 adaptation steps plus 200
    kept draws.
  - Each chain starts jittered by 0.25 prior SD about θ_ref's free coordinates.
  - The warm metric is diag((−H)⁻¹) at θ_ref over the variant's own free coordinates,
    with KIM-92's rule for the step and the floor.
  - Checkpoints every 5 iterations.
  - One extension: 200 more draws per chain, with no new adaptation, run only if the
    gate (§4) fails at 200.
- **Stage 2, each variant.**
  - **K = 200** stage-1 draws, evenly spaced through the pooled kept φ draws (after
    the extension, if one ran).
  - For each draw k, one NUTS(100, 0.8) chain at maximum tree depth 10 over ψ, keeping
    its **10** draws after adaptation.
  - Each inner chain starts at θ_ref's ψ, with a unit initial metric.
  - The result is **2000 cut draws** per variant: φ_k repeated 10 times beside its own
    ψ draws.
- **Seeds.** `95_000_000_000 + 64 · 17_200_001 + offset`, set by the driver, which
  refuses any that meet the dataset's cell, reference or KIM-92 chain seeds:
  - stage-1 chain c of variant v (1 = cut, 2 = plug-in): `offset = 1000 v + c`;
  - its extension: `offset = 1000 v + 10 + c`;
  - stage 2's inner chain k: `offset = 1000 v + 100 + k`.

## 4. The gates

These gate a variant's validity. They do not gate the project, since this is not a
kill check.

- **Stage-1 convergence.**
  - Pass: every φ coordinate has R̂ ≤ 1.02 and bulk ESS ≥ 100, over the 4 chains' kept
    draws, as in KIM-92's gate.
  - It is checked at 200 draws, then after the one extension if needed.
  - The cut's ψ coordinates are reported but not gated, since they are discarded.
- **A variant that fails after its extension** is reported as failed. Its stage 2 does
  not run, and what follows is Tom's call.
- **Stage 2 is reported, not gated.** For each inner chain: acceptance rate, numerical
  errors and tree depth. Inner chains whose kept draws hold a non-finite log density
  are counted.

## 5. The comparison with the joint (reported, not gated)

These are computed per variant against KIM-92's 1600 joint draws.

- **Shift.** For each of the 21 coordinates, (mean_cut − mean_joint) / SD_joint.
- **Contraction**, as in prereg/kim-92 §3 (1 − var_post/var_prior), beside the joint's.
- **"Close to the joint"** (frozen, Tom 2026-11-19). A variant is close when:
  - every coordinate's |shift| is ≤ 0.5; and
  - every coordinate's |contraction_cut − contraction_joint| is ≤ 0.05.

  The note reports which coordinates fail either test. It frames the note and KIM-97,
  and stops nothing.
- **Dropped coupling.** The correlations between ENO or FBA and each promoter, and with
  krnadeg, in the cut and in the joint.
- **Recovery**, as in prereg/kim-92 §3: the truth inside the central 90% interval, and
  the standardised error.
- **PPC**, KIM-92's predictive and band (prereg/kim-92 §2.2) on the cut draws.
  - The draws: 200 evenly spaced cut draws, with 10 replicates each.
  - Reported as the count of rows outside, out of 884, beside KIM-92's 11. It is not a
    gate here.
- **Cost.**
  - CPU-h by `sacct` per stage and variant: metric, chains, extension, stage 2 and PPC.
  - Wall-clock, and the critical path.
  - Set beside the joint's 491.9 CPU-h (Decision log 2026-11-17).

## 6. D3 scores (item 8): the plug-in variant only (Tom 2026-11-19)

- **What is scored.** The plug-in's cut draws, pushed forward by `predict` exactly as
  KIM-95 pushed the joint, with KIM-95's frozen sizes:
  - 100 draws, evenly spaced through the 2000;
  - 973 cells per draw;
  - both arms of do(ptsG), `Knockdown(dose = 0.3)` on `S_JCVISYN3A_0779`.
- **The targets and scores** are prereg/kim-92 §4's, as KIM-95 scored them, beside
  KIM-95's joint scores.
- **Reused from KIM-95's archive:** the truth runs and the prior arm
  (`infercell-kim95-archive-2026-11-19`).
- **The factor-cut variant is not scored.** It is compared on the posterior (§5) only.
