# KIM-180 pre-registration: doubling time, and the rule that picks the inferred block

Frozen at tag `prereg/kim-180` on 2026-11-28, before any prior-predictive draw of the
full cell had been simulated. This is a snapshot of the criteria, not a copy of the plan.
The plan lives in Linear (project InferCell, issues KIM-180, KIM-58 and KIM-186; Decision
log 2026-11-26 and 2026-11-28). A change to anything below after this tag is a Decision
log entry that quotes Was and Now, and says what had been read by then.

## 1. Doubling time

These are per cell, from SpeedyCell's per-minute record. The definitions are
syn3a-ensemble's `code/cell_cycle/events.py`.

- **t_SA2,** the cycle end. It is the first recorded minute at which `CellSA` is at least
  twice its minute-0 value. It is +∞ if that is not reached by the end of the window.
- **The window** is 180 simulated minutes (`minutes = 181`).
- **A cell is unhealthy** if, at or before its t_SA2 (or anywhere in the window if it does
  not reach t_SA2), it does either of these:
  - it collapses, which is the first minute with fewer than 59 hooks (t_short);
  - it ends in a recorded ODE failure, or throws.

  Every other cell is healthy. A cell that collapses after its t_SA2 has finished its
  cycle, so it is healthy.

Per prior draw, over its cells:

- **The doubling time T** is the ⌈n/2⌉-th smallest t_SA2 over the draw's n healthy
  cells. It is the median, with +∞ for a healthy cell censored at 180.
- **The collapse share** is the fraction of the draw's returned cells that are unhealthy.
  It is reported (§6). It does not enter the rule.

This is syn3a-ensemble's convention (`prereg/demo.md` §6), which takes t_SA2 in healthy
cells only, with one change. That file judges collapse over a 120-min window. Here
collapse is judged up to the cell's own cycle end, so a cell that divides and later
collapses is kept. Tom chose healthy cells only on 2026-11-28. Counting collapsed cells
as +∞ would fold viability into the ranking.

## 2. The design the rule is read on

The design is KIM-58's. These are the properties the rule needs from it:

- **Draws.** 10³ independent draws at each of three widths.
- **The prior.** Each named constant is multiplied by its own m_i, with log m_i ~ N(0, s²)
  independently.
  - The default width is a geometric SD of 2, so s = ln 2.
  - The half and double widths are geometric SDs of √2 and 4, so s = ½ ln 2 and 2 ln 2.
- **The named constants.** All 838 of SpeedyCell's: 805 ODE and 33 CME, with
  `param_names()` (`syn3a-ensemble/data/model/params/`). The multipliers go in through
  `Params(model; multipliers)`.
  - If KIM-57's prior table narrows the eligible set or gives the constants their own
    widths, that is a Decision log amendment to this section, made before KIM-58 runs.
- **Cells.** 100 per draw, each with its own procid and seed, run to minute 180. Cell
  seeds are distinct across draws.

## 3. Censoring, failures and missing cells

- **A draw with no healthy cell** is dropped from the ranking and counted. Its constants'
  effect shows in the collapse share (§6). Setting its T would fold viability back into
  the ranking.
- **A draw whose T is +∞** (half or more of its healthy cells censored at 180) is
  censored. Its T is set to 180.
- **Too many censored draws.** If more than 10% of the default width's ranked draws are
  censored, §4 is computed on the ranks of T instead of T, with the censored draws tied at
  the top rank. This fallback is fixed here, so it is not chosen after the data are seen.
- **A cell that throws** is listed. It counts as unhealthy (§1).
- **A draw with fewer than 90 returned cells** is dropped and counted.

## 4. The rule (Tom, 2026-11-27: 80%, a cap of 25, gSD 2, ties by the half and double widths)

The rule is read at the default width. Write u_i = log m_i for constant i, and T_d for
draw d's doubling time (§1, §3).

1. **The parameter variance.**
   - V = Var_d(T_d) − mean_d(v_d).
   - v_d is the Monte Carlo variance of draw d's median over its cells. It is estimated by
     bootstrapping the draw's cells (B = 200, seed 20261128).
2. **First-order indices,** by the given-data binning estimator.
   - For each i, sort the draws by u_i into 20 bins of equal count.
   - S_i = [Σ_b (n_b/N)(T̄_b − T̄)² − (19/N)·W_i] / V.
   - T̄_b is bin b's mean, N the number of draws, and W_i the pooled within-bin variance.
     The second term removes the between-bin variance that noise alone would give.
   - **The SE of S_i** comes from a bootstrap over draws: B = 1,000, seed 20261128, each
     draw resampled with all its cells.
3. **Eligible.** A constant is eligible if S_i exceeds the permutation null's threshold
   c, which controls false entries over all the constants together.
   - Permute T across the draws, breaking its link with u, and compute every S_i.
   - Record the largest S_i of that permutation. Repeat 1,000 times (seed 20261128).
   - c is the 95th percentile of those largest values.
   - Without this step the cap fills with noise. On a synthetic null at this N and
     number of constants, the top 25 binned S_i sum to 0.41
     (`dev/scripts/fullcell_doubling_kim180/null_sobol.py`).
4. **The block.**
   - Order the eligible constants by S_i, largest first.
   - The block is the smallest top k whose S_i sum to at least 0.80.
   - **The cap.** If 0.80 is not reached within 25, or not reached at all, the block is
     the top 25, or all the eligible constants if there are fewer. The shortfall, 0.80
     less the block's sum, is recorded.
5. **Ties at the edge.** The SE is §4.2's bootstrap SE.
   - If the k-th and (k+1)-th constants' S_i differ by less than 2 SE of their
     difference, the two are ranked by the mean of their S_i at the half and double
     widths, computed the same way.
   - If that leaves them within 2 SE again, both enter, and the cap becomes 26.

The block is what KIM-186 reports, and what the full-cell inference infers. Nothing else
enters the choice: not cost, not the inventory and not the route.

## 5. Checks before KIM-58's results are read

- **The definition on the default run.** §1 applied to the published parameter values
  has to give a doubling time inside Thornburg et al. 2022's range: 88 to 112 min, mean 97
  (Cell 185:345, Fig. 3F). It is checked twice:
  - on syn3a-ensemble's 10,000 default cells: 99 min;
  - on KIM-180's 100 default cells to minute 180 (job 21839731): 99 min.

  Both hold. The note is `dev/scripts/fullcell_doubling_kim180/doubling_result.md`.
- **The estimator on known answers.** KIM-186's implementation of §4 runs on synthetic
  T over 838 inputs at N = 10³, with noise at the measured v_d, in two cases. It runs
  before §4 is read on KIM-58, as KIM-186's first item.
  - **Sparse:** T additive in 5 inputs with known indices. It must recover each known
    S_i within 2 SE, and return those 5 as the block.
  - **Diffuse:** 5 inputs at 0.1 each, 50 at 0.004, the rest zero. At most 1 truly zero
    constant may be eligible, and the shortfall must be recorded.

## 6. Reported, not gated

- V, mean(v_d), the threshold c, the eligible constants' sum and the block's sum. The sum
  of all 838 S_i is not reported: its bias over that many constants swamps it.
- The S_i of the top 50, with their SE, at all three widths.
- The censored share and the collapse share, per width. The collapse share's own ranking
  is reported beside T's. It is not used.
- T at the 120-min cut, for comparison with syn3a-ensemble's window.
- The cost, in CPU-h, with the Slurm job ids.

First-order indices miss interactions, and at N = 10³ a constant below about 0.03 is not
resolved. If the eligible sum falls short of 0.80, the block carries less of the variance
than its target. That shortfall is reported, and it does not change the rule.
